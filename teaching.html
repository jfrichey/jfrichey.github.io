Courses · circles · mentoring

Learning by
doing mathematics.

My teaching has ranged from undergraduate probability, linear algebra, and calculus to middle-school math circles and undergraduate research. I like to begin with questions students can experiment with, then let the theory arrive when it becomes useful.

From 2016–2020, I co-led the second year of the UW Math Circle with Lucas van Meter. I have also worked with students through HCSSiM, WXML at the University of Washington, and REX at UBC. Some course pages, lesson ideas, and old materials are collected here for anyone who might find them useful.
