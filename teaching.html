Teaching

I have been a teacher and mentor for over 15 years, including as an college instructor for undergraduate and graduate courses, a leader of outreach programs for middle and high school students, and an advisor for undergraduate and masters students starting their research careers.

From 2016–2020, I co-led the second year of the UW Math Circle with Lucas van Meter. We often experimented with new ideas for interactive lessons on undergraduate math topics, decision theory, and games. I have mentored undergraduates and masters students with WXML at the University of Washington, REX at UBC, and at BME in Budapest. I have collected some links and teaching materials.
