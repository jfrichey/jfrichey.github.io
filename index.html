Jacob

I am a visiting researcher in the math department at NYU Shanghai, working with Alejandro Ramirez. My research is on combinatorial probability, including particle systems, random matrices, and dynamical systems.

From 2023–2026, I was a visiting researcher at the Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics in Budapest. From 2020–2023, I was a postdoc in the probability group at the University of British Columbia. Before that, I earned a PhD in Mathematics from the University of Washington and a BA in Mathematics and Chinese language from Dartmouth College.

Research

Random structures,
viewed in motion.

My research interests are in combinatorial probability and statistical physics. I work on shifts of finite type, interacting particle systems, random matrices, mixing times, random walks, stochastic geometry, and small questions that turn into unexpectedly large projects.

Teaching & mentoring

Mathematics is a

I have been the lead instructor for more than fifteen undergraduate courses in probability, linear algebra, and multivariable calculus. I have also taught at HCSSiM, led the second year of the UW Math Circle, and mentored undergraduate research through WXML and REX.
