Bridge

I learned bridge from a friend in college, and enjoyed competing both for Dartmouth and the University of Washington. A good deal of my time was spent recruting friends to join, and to help teach the game I wrote a few short notes to teach basic bridge bidding and some advanced cardplay situations. The notes are written in a mix of styles, like an article in The Bridge Bulletin, a well known periodical, and also with some attempts at humor like Bridge in the Menagerie, a famous series (by Victor Mollo, originally published in 1965).
