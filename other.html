01

A game worth learning

Bridge

I was once a member of the Dartmouth team (2012–2014) and then the University of Washington team (2014–2016). Bridge combines an auction, imperfect information, partnership communication, probability, and the occasional opportunity to execute something absurdly elegant.

The basic guide below is a short overview of bidding and card-play strategy written for friends and family. It includes an explicit decision tree for Standard American, one of the most common bidding systems.

Short, mildly comedic explanations

Rare card-play techniques

These essays were written to teach declarer and defensive plays that sound like moves from an especially baroque chess variant. Each technique is real; several are even useful more than once in a lifetime.

Start with the simple endplay or dummy reversal. Save the trump coup and two-suit squeeze for the moment when ordinary hobbies no longer provide enough terminology.

03

Favorites

Wu riddles

Wei-Hwa Huang and friends assembled a legendary collection of puzzles, lateral-thinking questions, and mathematical riddles. The original site has disappeared, but the archive remains an excellent place to lose an afternoon.

The best riddles have a solution that feels impossible until the instant it feels inevitable. They are also good training for research: identify the assumption everybody silently imported, and then remove it.
