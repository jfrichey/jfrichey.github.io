I am a recovering bridge addict. I played for Dartmouth and the University of Washington, and wrote a basic guide to help friends and family enter the game, together with short explanations of some rare and satisfying card-play techniques.

Wei-Hwa Huang and friends also assembled a legendary collection of puzzles, lateral-thinking questions, and mathematical riddles. The original site has disappeared, but the archive remains an excellent place to lose an afternoon.
